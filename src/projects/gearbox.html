Case Study

Compact Gearbox for E-Bike

Concept gearbox aimed at improving efficiency and ride feel for mid-drive e-bikes while keeping weight and envelope size low.

Problem

Existing drivetrain options were heavy or inefficient at high torque. The goal was to deliver a compact gearbox that could handle peak loads without sacrificing rider comfort.

Constraints

Package height limited to 85 mm, target weight under 1.8 kg (estimate), and noise targets below 55 dBA at nominal cruising torque (estimate).

System design

Evaluated planetary and parallel-axis layouts, selecting a two-stage helical train with a dog-clutch shift. Built a torque path model to understand bearing loads and lubrication needs.

Implementation

Modeled the gearbox in CAD, created efficiency spreadsheets, and designed a shifting cam to reduce pedal kickback. Drafted a serviceable housing split for easier assembly.

Results

Predicted system efficiency of 92–94% in cruise (estimate), with a 30% reduction in gearbox volume versus baseline concept (estimate).

What I’d improve next

Prototype the gear train for acoustic testing, run thermal simulations for continuous climbs, and refine the shift detent for improved tactile feedback.
